Privacy Policy — LinkedIn Content Studio  Last updated: October 18, 2026  ## 1. Introduction  LinkedIn Content Studio (“the App”) is a content research and drafting tool designed to help its operator discover publicly available articles, generate AI-assisted LinkedIn post drafts, organize content, and publish posts to LinkedIn after manual approval.  This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used and stored, and how you can request access to or deletion of your information.  ## 2. Information We Process  Depending on how you use the App, the following information may be processed:  - LinkedIn account information: LinkedIn member identifier and authorization credentials required to connect the App to LinkedIn. - Content information: Article titles, source URLs, publisher names, publication dates, generated drafts, edited posts, categories, approval statuses, planned dates, and publication records. - Technical information: Application configuration and error messages needed to operate and troubleshoot the App. - Third-party source information: Publicly available article information retrieved from configured RSS feeds.  The App does not intentionally collect LinkedIn account passwords.  ## 3. How We Use Information  Information is used to:  - Discover and organize publicly available content. - Generate AI-assisted drafts based on article information. - Save, edit, review, approve, and schedule content. - Connect to LinkedIn and publish posts when the operator explicitly requests publication. - Maintain content status and diagnose technical errors. - Protect and maintain the App.  The App is designed to require manual approval before publication. Generating or planning a post does not, by itself, publish it.  ## 4. AI Processing  The App uses Google's Gemini API to generate draft content from source information, including article titles, URLs, publisher names, publication dates, and available article snippets.  Information submitted to the AI provider is subject to that provider's applicable privacy policy and terms. Users should avoid including confidential or sensitive information in content submitted for AI processing. Google's policies and applicable API terms govern how information submitted through its services is processed.  ## 5. LinkedIn Authorization and Publishing  When you connect LinkedIn, the App uses LinkedIn's authorization process to obtain permission for the requested actions.  The App stores the LinkedIn access token in encrypted form in its database and stores the LinkedIn member identifier needed for publishing. Encryption depends on an application encryption key maintained separately in the App's configuration.  When the operator explicitly chooses to publish an approved post, the App sends the post content to LinkedIn through its API. LinkedIn then processes the content under its own privacy policy and terms.  The App does not sell LinkedIn access tokens or intentionally share them with unrelated parties.  You can revoke the App's access through LinkedIn's account settings. Revoking authorization may prevent further publishing until authorization is restored.  ## 6. Storage and Security  In the current local-development configuration, application records are stored in a database on the machine running the 

App. Access tokens are encrypted before being stored.  Reasonable safeguards include restricting access to application credentials, keeping secrets out of public source-code repositories, and limiting access to the database and application environment.  No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. The operator cannot guarantee absolute security.  If the App is deployed to a cloud provider, its storage, access controls, backups, and security configuration will depend on the actual deployment.  ## 7. Sharing and Third-Party Services  The App may interact with the following service providers:  - LinkedIn: For account authorization and publishing posts. - Google Gemini: For AI-assisted content generation. - Public RSS publishers: To retrieve publicly available article information. - Hosting and database providers: If the App is deployed to a cloud environment.  Information is shared with these services only as needed for the relevant functionality, subject to their applicable terms and policies. The App does not sell personal information.  ## 8. Retention and Deletion  Content records and application settings may remain in the database until they are deleted by the operator or removed through database maintenance.  To request deletion of information associated with your use of the App, contact the operator using the contact details below. Please do not include passwords, access tokens, or other secrets in your request.  Disconnecting LinkedIn removes the stored authorization credentials managed by the App, but it does not automatically delete posts already published to LinkedIn or all other application records. Published content must be managed through LinkedIn where applicable.  ## 9. Your Choices  You may:  - Decline to connect a LinkedIn account. - Revoke the App's authorization through LinkedIn. - Request deletion of information held by the App. - Stop using the App.  Revoking authorization does not undo posts already published.  ## 10. Children's Privacy  The App is intended for professional content management and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The operator does not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information.  ## 11. Changes to This Policy  This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality, data practices, or deployment changes. The updated version will be published at the same policy URL, together with a revised update date.  ## 12.
